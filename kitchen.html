Equipment Philosophy

Great cooking doesn't require expensive equipment. Skill, repetition, and understanding the process matter far more than buying more gear. Everything on this page is here because it has proven useful through repeated use—not because it's trendy.

Some links on this page may become affiliate links in the future. If that happens, I'll only recommend products I personally use and would confidently recommend to a friend.
