Sausage and Peppers

Sausage and peppers was a regular dinner in my house growing up, and every once in a while I find myself craving it. There's nothing fancy about this recipe—just good Italian sausage, peppers, onions, and a simple approach that lets each ingredient shine. For years I thought I didn't like cooked vegetables. Eventually I realized I just didn't like them overcooked.

Prep Time 10 minutes
Cook Time 25 minutes
Total Time 35 minutes
Servings 2–3

Watch the Video

Ingredients

  • 1 lb (450 g) Italian sausage links (sweet, hot, or mixed)
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 bell peppers (red, green, or mixed), sliced
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, sliced
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Crusty bread, for serving (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon of the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Brown the sausages on all sides until nearly cooked through. Transfer to a plate and slice if you like, or leave them whole.
  2. Add the remaining oil to the pan. Add the peppers and onion with a pinch of salt. Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until they soften but still have a little bite, 8–10 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
  4. Return the sausage to the pan and toss with the vegetables until everything is heated through and lightly glazed with the pan juices.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve as-is or with bread.

Chef’s Notes

I prefer my onions and peppers with a little bite left in them, and that's exactly how I make this dish. Don't cover the pan and steam them into submission. There's no tomato sauce here unless you want it—this version is sausage, peppers, and onions.
