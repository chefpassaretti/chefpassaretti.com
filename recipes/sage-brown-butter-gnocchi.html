Sage and Brown Butter Gnocchi

My sister picked up some fresh gnocchi from a local Italian deli and gave it to me to try. I made it for lunch that afternoon with one of the simplest sauces I know: brown butter and sage. The butter is cooked until golden and nutty while the sage turns crisp, then the gnocchi gets tossed directly into the pan with a little cooking water. Parmigiano-Reggiano finishes the sauce and brings everything together.

Prep Time 5 minutes
Cook Time 15 minutes
Total Time 20 minutes
Servings 2

Watch the Video

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (225 g) potato gnocchi
  • 1.5 tbsp (21 g) unsalted butter
  • 8–10 fresh sage leaves
  • ¾ oz (20 g) Parmigiano-Reggiano, finely grated
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Gnocchi cooking water, as needed
  • Extra Parmigiano-Reggiano, for serving

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook the gnocchi until they float, plus about 30 seconds. Reserve ½ cup of the cooking water.
  2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the sage and cook until the butter is golden and nutty and the leaves are crisp.
  3. Add the gnocchi and a splash of cooking water. Toss until the butter looks creamy and coats the gnocchi.
  4. Remove from the heat and toss in the Parmigiano-Reggiano.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with more cheese and the crisp sage.

Chef’s Notes

The balance on this one was incredible. The gnocchi had a great chew, the brown butter was rich without being heavy, the sage added just enough flavor, and the Parmigiano gave the sauce that slightly sticky, glossy finish.
