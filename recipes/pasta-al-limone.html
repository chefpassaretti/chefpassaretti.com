Pasta al Limone

Fresh lemon, butter, Parmigiano Reggiano, and pasta. Pasta al Limone is one of those recipes that proves you don't need a long list of ingredients to make something memorable. With a little attention to technique, these simple ingredients come together into a silky sauce that's bright, rich, and incredibly satisfying. This is a quick recipe for when you want something fresh and full of flavor without spending hours in the kitchen.

Prep Time 5 minutes
Cook Time 15 minutes
Total Time 20 minutes
Servings 2

Watch the Video

Ingredients

  • 8 oz (225 g) spaghetti
  • 3 tablespoons (42 g) unsalted butter
  • Zest of 1 large lemon
  • 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice, plus more to taste
  • ¾ cup (70 g) finely grated Parmigiano Reggiano, plus more for serving
  • Kosher salt
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Reserved pasta water, as needed

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water.
  2. Melt the butter in a large skillet over low heat. Add the lemon zest and a splash of pasta water.
  3. Add the pasta and toss, adding pasta water until the butter looks creamy.
  4. Remove from the heat. Add the lemon juice and Parmigiano Reggiano and toss continuously until the sauce is smooth and glossy.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve immediately with more cheese.

Chef’s Notes

This is brighter and cleaner than the brown-butter sage pasta. Temperature is the whole game—if the pan is too hot when the cheese goes in, it will seize. Warm pasta water and off-heat tossing are what make a short ingredient list work.

Equipment Used

  • Large pot
  • Large skillet
  • Microplane
  • Tongs
