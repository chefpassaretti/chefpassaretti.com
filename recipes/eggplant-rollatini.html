Eggplant Rollatini

Eggplant rollatini is one of those classic Italian dishes that turns a handful of simple ingredients into something incredibly satisfying. Tender slices of eggplant are rolled around a ricotta, Parmesan, garlic, and basil filling, then baked in marinara with mozzarella and Parmesan until everything is bubbling and golden. I love recipes like this because there isn't much to hide behind.

Prep Time 40 minutes
Cook Time 40 minutes
Total Time About 1 hour 20 minutes
Servings 2–3

Watch the Video

Ingredients

  • 1 large Italian eggplant (about 1¼ lb / 570 g)
  • Kosher salt
  • Olive oil, for roasting or frying
  • 1 cup (250 g) whole-milk ricotta
  • ½ cup (45 g) finely grated Parmigiano Reggiano, divided
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil, plus more for serving
  • 1 large egg
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 1½ cups (360 ml) marinara sauce
  • 4 oz (115 g) fresh mozzarella, sliced and drained

Instructions

  1. Slice the eggplant lengthwise into ¼-inch (6 mm) planks. Salt both sides and rest 20–30 minutes, then pat dry.
  2. Brush with olive oil and roast at 400°F (200°C) until pliable and lightly browned, 15–20 minutes. Or fry until tender. Cool until you can handle them.
  3. Mix the ricotta, half of the Parmigiano, garlic, basil, egg, salt, and pepper.
  4. Spread a thin layer of marinara in a baking dish.
  5. Place a spoonful of filling on each eggplant slice and roll it up. Nest the rolls seam-side down in the dish.
  6. Spoon over the remaining marinara. Top with mozzarella and the rest of the Parmigiano.
  7. Bake at 375°F (190°C) until bubbling and golden, 25–30 minutes.
  8. Rest 10 minutes. Finish with basil.

Chef’s Notes

Good eggplant, a simple cheese filling, homemade marinara, and a little patience are really all it takes. Salt and drain the eggplant so the rolls aren't watery. This is rolled and filled, not layered like the Parmesan.
