Privacy Policy — Chef Passaretti Analyzer

This application is used only by Chef Passaretti.

Contact:
chefpassaretti@gmail.com

Google Data It Accesses

With your consent, the app reads:

  • Your YouTube channel, upload list, video details, and published comment threads
  • YouTube Analytics reports for your channel, including views, watch time, impressions, click-through rate, and Suggested/Related traffic
  • Values from the Google Sheet you use as the Content Planner

It does not request permission to edit or delete YouTube videos, comments, ratings, or captions.

Why

The data is used to analyze your own channel, keep the Content Planner in sync with published videos, and produce your daily research brief and email.

Where It Is Stored

On the computer that runs the Analyzer, in local databases (analytics.db, demand_graph.db, and territory_discovery.db) and in a local OAuth token file. The token file is not stored in git.

Sharing

The app does not sell Google user data and does not send it to another product. The daily email is sent from chefpassaretti@gmail.com to that same address and contains the brief, not the OAuth token.

Retention

Collected rows stay in those local databases. The code does not run a scheduled purge of Google-sourced rows.

Revoking Access

You can revoke the app through your Google Account permissions:

https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

Revoking access stops new collection. Copies already stored on the computer remain until those files are deleted.

Limited Use

Google user data is used only to provide this private analysis to the Google account that authorized it, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.
