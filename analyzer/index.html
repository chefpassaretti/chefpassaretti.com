Chef Passaretti Analyzer

Chef Passaretti Analyzer is a private analytics and research application used by Chef Passaretti. It is not a public service, and it is not offered to other people.

It connects to Chef Passaretti’s own YouTube account. It reads channel, video, comment, and analytics information so the channel can be analyzed on a schedule. It reads the Chef Passaretti Content Planner from Google Sheets. It does not upload, edit, or delete YouTube videos, comments, ratings, or captions.

Scheduled jobs on Chef’s computer collect that information, store it locally, and send Chef a daily summary email.
